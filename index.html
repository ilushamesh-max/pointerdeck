Focused by design

The mouse controls you actually need.

PointerDeck is not a remote desktop. It does not stream or display the Mac screen. The release is built around pointer feel, clear click surfaces, scrolling, dragging, and returning to a trusted Mac.

Trackpad essentials

One-finger pointer movement, two-finger scrolling, dedicated left and right click, tap-to-click, and Drag Lock.

Set it to your hands

Adjust movement and scroll speed, choose Natural or Reversed scrolling, and switch to a left-handed layout.

Direct connection

Controller traffic goes between your devices and is encrypted on the local network. There is no PointerDeck cloud account.

Set up once

Your PIN opens a temporary door.

1

Open the receiver

At launch, download the free notarized Mac receiver, move it to Applications, and grant Accessibility so it can move and click the pointer.

2

Choose Add Device

Set an 8–12 digit Connection PIN. The Mac accepts it only while Add Device is open for up to two minutes.

3

Pair and return

Select the nearby Mac and enter its PIN. A separate trusted credential supports later reconnection when both apps are open and reachable.

Requirements

A Mac receiver is required.

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.
  • Mac running macOS 14 or later. Compatibility will be listed before download.
  • Both devices on the same local network.
  • Local Network permission on both devices and Accessibility on the Mac.
Network note: guest, public, corporate, or client-isolated Wi-Fi can prevent nearby devices from finding each other. PointerDeck is not designed for control over the public internet.
