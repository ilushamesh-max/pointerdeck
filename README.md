# PointerDeck

PointerDeck turns an iPhone or iPad into a focused trackpad for the Mac you can see. Move the pointer, click, scroll with two fingers, and move windows with Drag Lock without streaming the Mac screen.

[Download PointerDeck on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pointerdeck-trackpad-for-mac/id6806708304)

[Download the free Mac Receiver](https://ilushamesh-max.github.io/pointerdeck/mac.html)

## What you need

- iOS or iPadOS 17 or later
- macOS 14 or later
- The free PointerDeck Receiver running on the Mac
- Both devices on the same reachable local Wi-Fi network
- Local Network permission on iPhone or iPad
- Accessibility permission for PointerDeck Receiver on the Mac

PointerDeck is free and has no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or account. It controls the pointer locally and does not provide remote desktop, Bluetooth, or internet control.
