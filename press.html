PointerDeck press kit

PointerDeck is a free, focused iPhone and iPad trackpad for Mac. These approved facts and downloadable assets are available for editorial use.

View on the App Store Mac Receiver

One-line description

PointerDeck turns an iPhone or iPad into a focused local trackpad for a Mac.

Short description

PointerDeck lets people move, click, scroll, and drag on a Mac from an iPhone or iPad over the same local network. It uses a small, free Mac Receiver and is deliberately built without screen streaming, an account, ads, in-app purchases, or a subscription.

Important limitations

  • PointerDeck is not a remote desktop and does not show or stream the Mac screen.
  • It is not designed for public-internet control. Both devices must be on the same reachable local network.
  • Guest, hotel, public, corporate, or client-isolated Wi-Fi can prevent discovery.
  • App Store availability varies by region.
