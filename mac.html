Required Mac companion

PointerDeck Receiver for Mac

The receiver is the small Mac app that accepts trackpad commands from your iPhone or iPad. It is required, free, and does not add an account or subscription.

No public download is available yet. Only the final signed, notarized, stapled, clean-Mac-tested, and checksum-verified Receiver will be offered here.

Planned requirements

  • macOS 14 or later.
  • Final supported architecture shown beside the released download.
  • Same local network as the iPhone or iPad.
  • Accessibility permission for pointer movement, clicks, drags, and scrolling.

Final installation steps

  1. Download the versioned receiver from this HTTPS page.
  2. Verify the displayed filename, version, architecture, and SHA-256 when required by your organization.
  3. Move PointerDeck Receiver to the Applications folder and open it normally. A released build must not require a Gatekeeper bypass.
  4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and allow the exact PointerDeck Receiver copy in Applications.
  5. Set an 8–12 digit Connection PIN, choose Add Device, and pair from PointerDeck on the iPhone or iPad while the window is open for up to two minutes.

Why Accessibility is needed

macOS requires this permission before an app can post pointer, click, drag, and scroll events. PointerDeck Receiver does not use that permission to stream or display the Mac screen. If permission is denied, the receiver should remain available as a setup/status screen and explain how to return to System Settings.

Connection model

Controller traffic is encrypted on the local network. The Connection PIN is accepted only while the Mac owner opens Add Device. After pairing, a separate trusted-device credential stored in Keychain supports later reconnection. The numeric PIN design is not described as unbreakable or universally secure.

Final download record

The public release page will show one immutable versioned filename, receiver version/build, exact supported architecture, SHA-256, notarization status, Privacy Policy, and Support contact. Those values are intentionally absent until the final artifact exists.
